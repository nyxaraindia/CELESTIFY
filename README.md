# 🌌 CELESTIFY

> A tiny cosmic pet that grows with you—one song, one notification, and one focus session at a time.

**CELESTIFY** by **NYXARA_IN** brings Luna, a living cosmic companion, to your Android Dynamic Island and Windows 11 experience. Luna reacts to the rhythm of your day, evolves through atmospheric cycles, and builds a personal universe without requiring a constant internet connection.

## ✨ Meet Luna

Luna is more than a pet on your screen. She notices when you listen to music, receive notifications, or begin a focus session, then responds with a little life of her own.

- 🎵 **Music reactions** — Luna reacts to the sounds and energy around you
- 🔔 **Notification presence** — A gentle cosmic companion for important moments
- 🧘 **Focus sessions** — Calm, ambient support for intentional work
- 🌅 **Day–night cycles** — Luna's world changes throughout the day
- 📓 **Dream journals** — Capture memories and moments from Luna's universe
- 🏝️ **Social island visits** — Explore and share your evolving world
- 🔋 **Battery-smart behavior** — Designed to stay lightweight and efficient
- 📴 **Offline-first design** — Core experiences work without a cloud connection

## 🌍 A Living Universe

CELESTIFY is built around the idea that a companion should feel personal without feeling demanding.

```text
Your day
  ├── Music           → Luna dances, glows, or settles
  ├── Notifications   → Luna responds in the moment
  ├── Focus sessions  → Luna enters a calm, attentive state
  ├── Day & night     → The island transforms naturally
  └── Journal entries → Memories shape Luna's world
```

Over time, your everyday rituals contribute to an evolving island—making the experience yours without turning it into another task on your list.

## 🎯 Design Principles

- **Private by default** — Favor local-first data and user control
- **Low impact** — Preserve battery life and system resources
- **Context-aware** — React naturally to music, focus, and communication
- **Evolving, not demanding** — Growth happens alongside real life
- **Beautifully calm** — A companion should enhance focus, not compete with it
